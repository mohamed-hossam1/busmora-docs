## Part 1 --- Business & Opportunity

## 1. What We Do --- One-page Business Introduction

We help small-business owners, particularly solo founders, take the next
step in building and growing their businesses.

Many startups and early-stage businesses have promising ideas, capable
teams, and strong products, yet can't afford to partner with a marketing
agency or hire a senior marketing director.

BusMora solves this problem by not only providing all the marketing
tools your business is gonna need, but also providing the how and the
why behind every tool.

During onboarding, we collect your website and social media links and
allow you to upload your business documents. We extract the relevant
information from these sources to understand your business. Then,
BusMora's AI Marketing Director diagnoses your business, builds a
business plan if you don't already have one, identifies the best next
step to take, determines which tools it needs and in what order to use
them, and presents the proposed step for your approval before executing
it.

The problem was never a lack of tools, but rather knowing which tools to
use, when to use them, and how.

BusMora takes care of that burden for you and lets you focus more on
your product and your sales.

## 3. Industry Overview

### Industry Definition

BusMora operates in the AI-powered Marketing Technology (MarTech)
industry. This industry uses software, data, and artificial intelligence
to help businesses plan, create, manage, and execute their marketing
activities. BusMora brings these capabilities together under one AI
Marketing Director that determines what needs to be done and executes
approved work.

### Market Opportunity

Egypt's business formation data shows significant growth in newly
established companies. According to GAFI, Egypt registered **5,525 new
companies in September 2026**, compared with **3,655 in September
2025**, representing a **51.2% increase**. Within the Information and
Communications Technology sector, **477 new companies** were established
in September 2026, compared with **229 in September 2025**, representing
growth of **108.3%**.

**Evidence:** [GAFI Monthly Investment Report --- September
2026](https://gafiadmin.gafi.gov.eg/style-library/ExtractedVirtualLibraries/8317/9afb1e56d1384a21b353763ccabb0cfb/Monhly%20Report%20-%20September%2026/GAFI%20Monhly%20Report%20-%20September%2026.pdf)

The Egyptian Digital Marketing and AdTech market is estimated at **\$1.5
billion in 2025** and **\$1.72 billion in 2026**, representing estimated
annual growth of **14.67%**. The market is projected to reach **\$3.77
billion by 2032**, with a forecast CAGR of **14.07%** between 2025 and
2032.

**Evidence:** [Ken Research --- Egypt Digital Marketing & AdTech
Market](https://www.kenresearch.com/egypt-digital-marketing-and-adtech-market)

### Why Now?

The growth in newly established companies, combined with the expansion
of the digital marketing industry, creates an opportunity for businesses
that support startups during their early stages. New founders need more
than access to marketing tools; they need to know what to do, when to do
it, and how to execute it effectively.

Similar to how NVIDIA provides the technology and infrastructure that AI
companies need to build their products, BusMora aims to provide startups
with the marketing capabilities, direction, and execution they need to
maximize their potential. Rather than building another startup competing
for success in a single industry, we aim to build a business that
supports many startups in achieving their goals while generating profit
from their growth.

### Scalability

BusMora's AI-driven model allows us to serve multiple startups through
one platform without requiring a separate marketing team for every
customer. This creates the potential to scale across Egypt and
eventually expand into other Arabic-speaking markets, including the GCC.

### 4.2 Product Capabilities & Current Development Status

BusMora's core capabilities include:

1.  **Business Understanding:** During onboarding, BusMora collects
    relevant business information from the startup's website, social
    media accounts, and uploaded business documents.
2.  **Business Diagnosis:** It analyzes the collected information to

4.  **Next-Step Identification:** It identifies the best next step for

5.  **Proposed Execution:** It presents a proposal to the founder and
    waits for approval before executing the proposed work.

### 4.3 Solution Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths** - Specifically designed for fresh startups and early-stage
founders. - Combines business diagnosis, marketing direction, and
execution. - Determines the next best step instead of leaving founders
to figure it out themselves. - Provides a connected marketing workflow
rather than isolated tools. - Keeps founders in control through approval
before execution.

**Weaknesses**

Our current free tier is not very generous. We believe that offering a
more generous free tier could allow founders to experience more of
BusMora's value, making a paid subscription an obvious choice once they
have the financial resources to subscribe.

However, expanding the free tier must be balanced against AI usage and
operating costs.

### 4.4 Cost-Based Pricing Strategy

Our pricing strategy will be based on the actual cost of acquiring and
serving each customer.

Our initial working formula for the monthly cost per user is:

**Monthly cost per user = Monthly token-consumption cost + (Customer
Acquisition Cost / Average Customer Lifetime)**

Where: - **Monthly token cost:** The average cost of the user's monthly
AI token consumption. - **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** The cost of
acquiring that user through advertisements, outreach, cold calls, or any
other acquisition channel. - **L (Average Customer Lifetime):** The
expected number of months the customer will use BusMora before
transitioning to a dedicated in-house marketing team or a marketing
agency.

The customer acquisition cost is distributed across the customer's
expected lifetime on BusMora to determine its monthly contribution to
the cost of serving that customer.

**Pending calculations:** - Initial development and setup costs. -
Average daily and monthly token consumption per user. - Average monthly
AI consumption cost. - Customer acquisition cost across different
channels. - Expected customer lifetime. - Other operating and
infrastructure costs.

Final subscription prices will be determined after these costs are
established.

## Part 2 --- Business Model & Execution

## 5. Business Model --- Business Model Canvas

### 5.1 Customer Segments

BusMora aims to serve small businesses, particularly those operating in
the ICT sector. The initial customer focus is fresh startups and
early-stage founders.

### 5.2 Value Propositions

BusMora does not position itself as simply selling marketing tools or
AI. It offers **intelligence, guidance, clarity, and freedom from
distraction (عدم التشتت)**.

Instead of leaving founders to figure out what to do next, which tools
to use, and how to use them, BusMora provides direction and a guided
path that helps them move their businesses forward.

The product is what BusMora builds; the value proposition is the benefit
customers receive: clarity, direction, and less distraction so they can
focus on building and growing their businesses.

### 5.3 Customer Relationships

BusMora will maintain an ongoing relationship with customers through
weekly feedback requests and surveys. These will ask users whether they
have faced problems while using the product and whether they have ideas
for features that would help them more.

The feedback will inform product improvements and help BusMora respond
to actual customer needs.

### 5.4 Channels

BusMora will test multiple customer acquisition channels in parallel,
evaluate their performance, and then decide which channels deserve the
most focus.

Initial channels: - **Direct outreach:** Cold calls, emails, and
WhatsApp messages. - **Original content:** Creating original content to
attract and educate potential customers. - **Paid advertising:** To be
introduced later, when sufficient resources are available.

### 5.5 Revenue Streams

BusMora will primarily generate revenue through monthly subscriptions.
Each subscription will include a weekly usage limit that renews every
week. Customers who need additional usage beyond their weekly allowance
will be able to purchase additional credits.

### 5.6 Cost Structure

Current cost categories identified: - AI token and API consumption. -
Customer acquisition through outreach, cold calls, email, WhatsApp,
original content, and later paid advertising. - Initial development and
setup costs. - Hosting, infrastructure, and other operating expenses.

The working monthly per-user cost formula is:

**Monthly cost per user = Monthly token-consumption cost + (CAC /
Average Customer Lifetime)**

Initial costs, average user consumption, acquisition cost, and customer
lifetime remain to be measured or calculated before final pricing and
margin decisions.
