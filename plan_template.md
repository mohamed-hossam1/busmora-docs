# BusMora Business Plan Template

## Part 1 --- Business & Opportunity

### 1. What We Do --- One-page Business Introduction

-   What is BusMora?
-   What problem are we solving?
-   What's our solution?
-   Who is our target audience?
-   Who are the founders?

### 2. Where We Stand --- Current Business Stage

-   Current stage: idea, prototype, MVP, or first customer.
-   What have we achieved?
-   What remains to be done?
-   Why do we need incubation or funding?

### 3. Industry Overview

-   Industry definition (2--3 lines).
-   Market size and growth rate.
-   Size of the opportunity.
-   Scalability potential.
-   Why is now a good time to enter this industry?

### 4. Product & Solution

-   Problem and customer pain, supported by numbers.
-   Product capabilities and current development status.
-   Solution strengths and weaknesses.
-   Cost-based pricing strategy.
-   Competitor pricing considerations.
-   Expected profit margins.

## Part 2 --- Business Model & Execution

### 5. Business Model --- Business Model Canvas

All nine components:

1.  Customer Segments
2.  Value Propositions
3.  Customer Relationships
4.  Channels
5.  Revenue Streams
6.  Cost Structure
7.  Key Partners
8.  Key Resources
9.  Key Activities

**Important distinction:** The product is what we build; the value
proposition is the benefit customers receive and why that benefit is
worth paying for.

### 6. Milestones --- Monthly Roadmap

-   What will we accomplish every month?
-   Technical development milestones.
-   Business and commercial milestones.
-   Expected deliverables and measurable results.

### 7. Market Research

-   Surveys and their results.
-   Customer interviews.
-   Identified problems and needs.
-   Evidence of demand.
-   Quantitative findings and conclusions.

## Part 3 --- Market Position & Team

### 8. Target Market & Segmentation

-   Who exactly are we targeting now?
-   Customer segments and geography.
-   Total potential market.
-   TAM, SAM and SOM.
-   The difference between the overall market and our reachable initial
    market.

### 9. Competitor Analysis & Benchmarking

-   Direct competitors.
-   Indirect competitors.
-   Competitor comparison by:
    -   Product and features.
    -   Price.
    -   Quality.
    -   Competitive advantages.
-   Include BusMora in the comparison.
-   Explain our competitive positioning.

### 10. Team Structure

-   Founders and team members.
-   Responsibilities and assignment ownership.
-   Relevant experience.
-   Existing skills.
-   Missing skills and how we intend to address them.

## Part 4 --- Financials & Incubation

### 11. Financial Plan

-   Historical revenue and expenses.
-   Projected revenue.
-   Projected expenses.
-   Financial assumptions.
-   Use of funding.
-   Financial risks.

### 12. Supporting Documents & Evidence

-   Commitments and deals.
-   Interviews and discussions.
-   Recommendations.
-   Achievements and verification.
-   MVP or product demonstration link.
-   Client feedback and comments.
-   Relevant supporting documents.

### 13. Incubation Objectives & Use of Funding

-   Why do we need incubation?
-   How much funding are we requesting?
-   What will we do with every Egyptian pound?
-   How will the funding translate into milestones?
-   What measurable results will the incubator receive in return?
